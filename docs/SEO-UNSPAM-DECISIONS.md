# SEO de-spam decisions (2026-11-11)

Audit run with the claude-seo plugin methodology (v2.6.0: seo-programmatic thin-content
gates, seo-content hidden-text check, seo-schema deprecated-type list) against the
repository and the live site. This file records what was found, what was decided, and
how to tell whether each decision worked. Totals after the change: sitemap 752 to 569 URLs, 208 redirects in
`public/_redirects` (each emitted with and without a trailing slash).

## Findings (measured, not assumed)

| # | Signal | Evidence | Google policy it trips |
|---|--------|----------|------------------------|
| 1 | 9,261 keyword-permutation alias URLs (`src/data/tool-extraslugs.json`) rendered as standalone pages with keyword-swapped H1, title, article and FAQs | `(intent)/[slug]` alias branch; 593 tools x ~15 slugs | Doorway pages |
| 2 | 148 "SEO opportunity" long-tail pages, 133 self-canonical and in the sitemap; median 80% of each page's prose shared with siblings (109 of 148 above 60%) | 6-gram shingle analysis, `scripts/audit-content-uniqueness.ts` | Scaled content abuse, doorway pages |
| 3 | 640 tool articles labelled "manually crafted" share a median 87% of their 6-word phrases (519 of 640 above 60%) | same script | Scaled content abuse |
| 4 | `VisuallyHiddenSeoContext`: an `sr-only` block of SEO prose on every tool page | `ToolLayout.tsx` | Hidden text |
| 5 | Templated fallback article, features, steps and FAQ generated at render time for any tool lacking content | `ToolLayout.tsx`, `StructuredData.tsx` | Scaled content abuse |
| 6 | JSON-LD on every tool page: `Article` (wrong type for a tool), `HowTo` (rich result retired 2023), `FAQPage` with templated fallback questions | `StructuredData.tsx`, live check of /bmi-calculator (14 JSON-LD blocks) | Structured data guidelines (markup must match visible, meaningful content) |
| 7 | `/tool-markdown/<id>` serves the same prose as text/markdown for all 640 tools, indexable, 200 live | live HEAD check | Duplicate content |
| 8 | Duplicate tools under two URLs with the identical component (gst-calculator vs gst-calculator-india, cgpa-to-percentage vs -india, marks-needed vs required-marks, freelance-rate vs freelance-hourly-rate, passport-photo-maker vs -india) | page.tsx comparison | Doorway / duplicate |
| 9 | 38 of 100 blog posts under 180 words; 41 posts dated 2026-09-20 and 25 dated 2026-07-12 | `scripts/audit-blog-thinness.ts` | Thin content, scaled content |
| 10 | 404 page emits both `noindex` and `index, follow` robots meta | live fetch of an alias URL | Correctness |

Blog prose is unique across posts (shared phrase ratio under 1%), so the blog problem is
thinness, not templating.

## Decisions

1. **Delete the alias slug system** (finding 1). No redirects: the URLs already return 404
   on production and were never in the sitemap. Code, data and tests referencing
   `extraSlugs`/`getToolByExtraSlug` are removed.
2. **Consolidate opportunity pages** (finding 2). Keep a page only when it delivers a
   function the parent tool does not offer by default (exam-specific photo/signature
   presets, WiFi and UPI QR payloads, cm/mm resize, DPI). Numeric permutations
   (`compress-image-to-NNkb`, `compress-pdf-to-NN`), GPA/grade synonyms, API-key
   provider twins without provider-specific logic, SEO-check pages whose tool does not
   do what the URL says, and `-online`/`-free` synonym pairs are retired with a 301 to the
   parent tool. Exception kept: `compress-image-to-20kb` and `compress-image-to-50kb`
   (highest-volume form-upload queries), rewritten with preset-specific prose.
   Survivors: 18 entries (5 with their own URL: compress-image-to-50kb, ssc-signature-resizer,
   upsc-signature-resizer, qr-code-for-wifi, qr-code-for-upi-payment; 13 whose slug is a
   real tool route and whose prose renders on that tool page). Retired: 130, with 128
   redirects (two retired slugs are real tool routes and keep their tool page).
3. **Gate prose behind a measured uniqueness allowlist** (findings 3 and 5). Tool and
   opportunity pages render article, features, steps and FAQ only when the page's
   6-gram shared ratio is below 0.4 and it has at least 120 words
   (`src/data/content-uniqueness.json`, regenerated by `bun run audit:content`). All
   render-time fallback boilerplate is deleted. Non-curated pages are tool-first: H1,
   one description, the tool, related tools. Curated now: 119 of 603 tools and 6 of 18 opportunities.
4. **Remove hidden text** (finding 4). `VisuallyHiddenSeoContext` deleted with its test.
5. **Trim structured data** (finding 6). Tool pages emit `SoftwareApplication` and
   `BreadcrumbList`; `FAQPage` only for curated FAQs; `Article` and `HowTo` removed.
6. **Noindex the markdown mirror** (finding 7). `public/_headers` sets
   `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `/tool-markdown/*`; the mirror carries prose only for
   curated tools.
7. **Merge duplicate tools** (finding 8) with 301s in `src/seo/redirects/tools.json`.
   37 routes retired in total: the five pairs above, /youtube-video-downloader, and 31
   downloader/alias slugs that rendered the identical component with no props (for
   example /save-tiktok-mp4, /ig-reels-downloader-hd, /fb-clip-downloader). Registry: 640
   to 603 tools. Kept routes for the named pairs: /gst-calculator,
   /cgpa-to-percentage-calculator, /required-marks-calculator,
   /freelance-hourly-rate-calculator, /passport-photo-maker, /youtube-downloader.
8. **Retire thin blog posts** (finding 9). Every post under 180 words is removed and
   301s to the tool it promoted (or /blog). Nothing is padded with generated text.
   Retired: 38 (31 to the promoted tool, 7 to /blog). Remaining: 62. Blog pagination
   shrank from 9 to 6 pages; /blog/page/7-9 redirect to /blog.
9. **Fix the 404 robots conflict** (finding 10).

Redirects live in `src/seo/redirects/*.json` and are compiled into `public/_redirects`
by `scripts/generate-redirects.cjs` (the site is a static export, so `next.config`
redirects do not apply).

## Falsifiability and leading indicators

- If decision 2 or 8 was wrong, Search Console will show lost clicks on the retired
  URLs that do not reappear on the redirect target within 4 to 6 weeks. Re-add a
  specific page only with its own distinct function and prose.
- If decision 3 was wrong, curated pages will not outperform non-curated ones on
  impressions per page after the next core update. The allowlist threshold (0.4) is a
  single number in the audit script.
- Leading indicators to watch weekly in Search Console: "Crawled, currently not
  indexed" count (should fall), "Duplicate without user-selected canonical" (should
  fall), average position of the surviving opportunity pages (should hold or rise).

## Not changed (deliberately)

- Blog posts at or above 180 words, even where generic. They are unique; rewriting at
  scale would recreate the problem.
- The `/archive` directory page and category hubs.
- AI crawler allowances in robots.
- Ad slots (`SHOW_SCRIPTLY_ADS` stays false).
